About Boundless

Boundless is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to education on human sexuality, healthy relationships, consent, and communication. Our educational classes and social events provide individuals the opportunities for self-discovery and personal growth, in order to help them find and build community, and strengthen their own interpersonal connections.

Our Mission

Boundless exists to create and nurture a diverse, inclusive, and safe community where people are free to express love, intimacy, and connection.

Our Vision

Our vision is to offer education and events that foster personal growth and interpersonal connection for our community, and to collaborate with other organizations that share our values.

Our Values

Boundless strives to embody these values, and to exhibit them in everything that we do:

  • Integrity
  • Service
  • Education
  • Inclusion
  • Diversity
  • Consent

What We Do

Educational Classes

We organize educational classes, discussion groups, and skill-sharing opportunities that allow individuals to learn more about consent, human sexuality, relationship formats, communication, and related topics. Since July 2020 many of our classes have been offered virtually, which has expanded our outreach.

Community Building

We organize happy hours, meetups, and picnics that bring members of our community together in safe and non-sexual environments to make new connections, grow friendships, and build their own communities.

The Boundless Retreat

Our annual four-day retreat in Northern California combines educational workshops and skill-sharing with catered meals and community activities. Attendees can propose and lead their own one-hour sessions through our peer-led, un-conference style program, Boundless YOU•NIVERSITY.

Scholarship Program

We realize that there are barriers to attending for some people, who may face additional obstacles in their lives. Each year we offer a limited number of scholarship admissions to the Retreat, reviewed by our Organization Committee, so that our events are accessible and reflect the full breadth of our community.

We strive to make all of our events more accessible and to find opportunities for equity and inclusion. We believe that when our events are attended by a wide range of people and their individual perspectives, experiences, and wisdom, we all benefit.

Born Out of Community

The concept of Boundless came from another invitation-only camping event that our founder helped organize for a number of years. In 2015 our founder established the Boundless Events organization as a way to serve the broader Northern California community by producing an annual retreat.

In each of the following years, the Boundless Retreat and community continued to grow. Boundless soon began hosting happy hours and meetups so the community could gather year-round, get to know each other, and grow the friendships and bonds that were established during the retreat.

Like many other organizations, the onset of the COVID pandemic was a difficult challenge for Boundless and our community. However, through innovation and creativity Boundless began offering our educational classes virtually in July 2020, which has expanded our outreach.

Code of Conduct

At Boundless we strive to create a space for community and events that is safe and inclusive and allows people to be themselves. While we do everything we can to promote our culture of consent and provide the framework, this also depends on everyone who attends our events. As a member of the Boundless community, we expect you to conduct yourself in accordance with three simple principles:

1. Take Responsibility for Yourself

We expect you to think about what you need, plan for your needs, and take care of yourself. Only you can fully understand what your comfort levels are, and we ask you to come to our events as prepared as you can be. If you need help, please reach out to those who can support you — and to us. We expect you to take responsibility for your level of intoxication, and to make sure you are never too intoxicated to behave responsibly toward the community and to care for yourself.

2. Take Responsibility for Your Behavior Toward Others

We expect you to treat everyone you meet with kindness and consideration. As part of our community, we expect you to promote anti-racism, anti-sexism, and gender equality, and to consider social power dynamics when interacting with other community members. You must adhere to our consent policies in all of your interactions with individuals attending our events.

3. Take Responsibility for the Venue

It is a privilege to use the spaces where we gather, and it is on us to take care of them. We are guests and must treat our hosts and venues with regard; our actions will impact future use of these unique locations.

If something doesn't seem right, you are encouraged and empowered to speak up or report that behavior to the organizers or event volunteers. The code of conduct is a shared belief: Boundless and our event organizers will do whatever is necessary to keep our events a healthy and safe environment for all attendees, and may remove those who are unable to adhere to these agreements.

Privacy at Our Events

Boundless maintains a strong culture of privacy. Aside from a designated staff photographer who photographs only those who ask, photography and video recording by attendees is not permitted at our events. Anyone posting a photo to social media must first have the consent of everyone in it.

Consent Principles

Boundless is a consent-focused organization in everything we do. Our Consent Policy defines the practices we encourage among all community members, based on the ethical principle that everything we do together is done by informed agreement among all of the participants.

  • Consent must be freely given. People giving consent must do so voluntarily, without being subjected to pressure, threats, manipulation, fraud, coercion, or deceit. To consent you must be in a clear-headed state of mind, not impaired by intoxication.
  • Consent is revocable. Anyone can withdraw consent to anything at any time.
  • Consent is informed. Everyone involved must have the information they need about an activity and its risks to make an informed decision.
  • Consent is enthusiastically given. Affirmative consent is the presence of a yes, not the absence of a no, and is given on an opt-in basis.
  • Consent is given within specific limits. Consent is not a blank check, and is never assumed for anything that has not been openly discussed and agreed to by everyone involved.

Above all, consent is communication: there should always be a mutually understood means of communication between everyone involved.

Feedback & Accountability

Boundless is committed to responding to feedback about the conduct of community members. Our Conduct Committee is the part of the Boundless team responsible for addressing problems or potential problems within our events, and those reported to us by others in the broader community. In our policies and procedures we make every effort to honor the values of safety, accountability, restorative healing, privacy, and transparency.

Making a report

Anyone who was directly affected by, or who witnessed, behavior that was unsafe or harmful may make a report at any time by emailing conduct@boundlessevents.org, or during an event by contacting any organizer, host, or member of the Conduct Committee on duty. All reports are handled equitably, regardless of who is involved or that person's role within Boundless.

How reports are handled

  • The Conduct Committee documents each report and contacts the reporter to discuss it and the possible outcomes.
  • Investigators speak with witnesses and with the subject of the report. No action is recommended without first interviewing the person the report is about.
  • The Committee reaches consensus on a recommendation — ranging from a conversation or an official warning, to limits on volunteering, to a ban from events — which the Organization Team accepts, investigates further, or adjusts.
  • Reporters' identities and details are never revealed without their explicit consent, and no retaliatory action is taken against anyone for reporting, or not reporting, a problem in good faith.

Conduct Committee members are bound by a written confidentiality policy, and their volunteer positions are contingent on adhering to it.

A Volunteer Organization

Boundless is a volunteer organization at all levels, and we rely on the strength of our community and the generous hearts of our volunteers to produce the educational and community events that we offer. From Food to Finance, Classes to Conduct, and Activities to Admin, volunteers can help with a single event or take a larger role in helping grow our organization and community.

Community Engagement

Expands the Boundless presence in the Bay Area, organizing our happy hours and meetups and coordinating hosts.

Events

Oversees the planning and execution of our events and the Boundless Retreat, including contracts, venues, and project management.

Food

Coordinates the food offered at our events, including catering services for the Boundless Retreat.

Medical

Ensures Boundless has the appropriate medical personnel and coverage to support our events and the Retreat.

To get involved, contact our Volunteer Coordinator at volunteers@boundlessevents.org.
